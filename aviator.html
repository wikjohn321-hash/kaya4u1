Aviator on Kaya4U: how the crash game works

Aviator is a crash game. A plane takes off and a multiplier rises, and you win if you cash out before the plane flies away.

How a round works

Before each round you place a bet. When the round starts, the multiplier climbs from 1.00x. Tap cash out at any moment to take your bet multiplied by the current number. If the plane leaves before you cash out, the bet is lost.

Because the moment of the crash is unpredictable, no method can guarantee a win. Treat every round as a game of chance.

Playing with a plan

  • Set a budget for the session before you open the game.
  • Choose a bet size that lets you survive several losing rounds.
  • Decide in advance when you will stop, whether you are ahead or behind.
  • Never chase losses with bigger bets.
