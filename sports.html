Understanding a bet

A sports bet is a prediction, such as which team wins a match. The odds show how much you receive if you are right. Higher odds mean a lower chance of winning. Read the bet slip carefully before you confirm.

Tips for NRL fans

  • Bet on matches you know, not just on matches that are available.
  • Check injury news and team lists before the game.
  • Do not bet on your own team out of loyalty alone.

Keep it affordable

Set a weekly budget and stay inside it. Do not chase a loss with a bigger bet, and stop if betting stops being fun. Odds and markets are shown on the platform and can change before the match.
