Kaya4U referral program: 1% and 0.5% explained

Kaya4U advertises its referral program as: 1% from your friends plus 0.5% from their friends, on every bet, credited automatically.

What the numbers mean

  • 1% of the bets made by friends you invite.
  • 0.5% of the bets made by the friends they invite in turn.
  • Rewards are described as automatic, so you do not need to claim them each time.

These are the advertised rates. The Agent page on Kaya4U shows the rates and conditions that apply to your account, and those are the ones that count.

How to get started

  1. Register on Kaya4U and log in.
  2. Open the Agent page from the bottom menu.
  3. Share your invite link with people you know.

Please invite responsibly

Only invite adults, 18 or over, and be honest that gambling carries risk and can lose money. A referral reward depends on what other people bet, so never treat it as guaranteed income.
